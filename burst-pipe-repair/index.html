Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Garland Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Garland and surrounding areas. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A musty smell near a wall, cabinet or floor with plumbing behind it
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Unheated spaces like garages, attics and crawl spaces need pipe insulation on exposed lines
